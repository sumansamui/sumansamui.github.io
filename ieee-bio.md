Suman Samui (Member, IEEE) received the Ph.D. degree from the Indian Institute of Technology Kharagpur, Kharagpur, India, in 2019.,He is an Assistant Professor with the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering, National Institute of Technology Durgapur, Durgapur, India. Prior to this role, he worked with IIEST Shibpur, Howrah, India, from 2020 to 2022, and NIT Rourkela, Rourkela, India, from 2019 to 2020, as a Guest Faculty with the Department of Electronics and Communication Engineering. He is also worked as a Postdoctoral Researcher for six months at the Idiap Research Institute, Martigny, Switzerland, in 2018. He has authored or coauthored more than 20 research papers in reputed international conferences and peer-reviewed journals. His research interests encompass audio/speech signal processing, digital very large scale integration design, and applications based on machine learning and deep learning.
